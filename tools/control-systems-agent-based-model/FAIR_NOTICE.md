# FAIR Methodology Notice

## About FAIR and FAIR-CAM

This software implements concepts from the **Factor Analysis of Information Risk (FAIR)** methodology and the **FAIR Controls Analytics Model (FAIR-CAM)**, frameworks for cyber risk quantification and control effectiveness analysis.

## Implementation Notes

This implementation follows the FAIR-CAM specification as documented in:
- *"Measuring and Managing Information Risk: A FAIR Approach"* by Jack Jones and Jack Freund
- The FAIR-CAM technical standard published by the FAIR Institute

Any deviations from or extensions to the standard FAIR-CAM methodology are documented in the accompanying paper and in this repository.
